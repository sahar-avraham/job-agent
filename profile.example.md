## Rubric

Report fit, desirability and odds, each 0 to 100, then the overall score.

**Fit** is how well the day-to-day work matches what the candidate can already do.

**Desirability** is whether they would take the job. It is reported and not weighted.

**Odds** is whether a recruiter would plausibly call them in. Read the stated
experience as a floor:

- No figure, or a floor of one year: start at 80.
- A floor of two years: start at 65.
- A floor of three years: start at 35.
- A floor of four or five years: start at 20.
- Six years or more: start at 5.

Lower it further only for a mandatory demand that cannot be learned on the job,
such as a clearance or years inside a specific industry. An unfamiliar language or
framework is not such a demand.

The overall score is 70 percent odds and 30 percent fit.
